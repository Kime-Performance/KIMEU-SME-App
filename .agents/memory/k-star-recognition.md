name: Optional K-Star recognition
description: KB Resilience awards are optional, highest-earned, and shared through the Master sheet.
---

K-Star recognition is optional for KB Resilience only, independent of workshop/pathway completion. Preserve each clinician's highest valid earned award, not merely the latest retest. Historical blanks must not be interpreted as credentials.

**Why:** The user's K-Star spec explicitly requires lower/zero retests and unchecked edits to retain earlier awards, without changing KB/Fellowship completion.

**How to apply:** Keep ordinary sign-offs and unrelated edits from clearing award evidence. Do not append award-only workshop rows: the downstream app uses the most recently updated course row for completion evidence.

RD practical and K-Star entry must be clearly discoverable for a selected, eligible PT, rather than hidden behind generic Assess/Reassess wording.

**Why:** The user reported that neither the RD practical completion action nor the K-Star earned option could be found after implementation.

**How to apply:** Use explicit action labels and explain saved-attendance prerequisites. Keep the optional K-Star result selector visible in its form, disabled until the performed-test checkbox is checked; never silently preselect an award or weaken access checks.

The user's supplied consumer contract says the separate Clinician Development app already reads retained KB rows directly from the Master, recognizes the highest qualifying award, and may take about 5–6 minutes to refresh. Its public site also needs its own K-Star build published.

**Why:** The two projects have separate publishing and refresh behavior, even though they share the sheet.

**How to apply:** Distinguish SME form/sheet verification from cross-app end-to-end verification. Do not claim that the other app's public display was tested from this workspace or fabricate awards for real clinicians.
