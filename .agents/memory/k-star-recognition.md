**Why:** The two projects have separate publishing and refresh behavior, even though they share the sheet.

**How to apply:** Distinguish SME form/sheet verification from cross-app end-to-end verification. Do not claim that the other app's public display was tested from this workspace or fabricate awards for real clinicians.
